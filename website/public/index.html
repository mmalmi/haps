Publish with a key.

No registry account or approval queue. Your public key identifies you, and package names belong to their publishers.

Shared peer to peer.

Hashtree distributes software by content hash. Anyone can host it; Haps checks signatures and hashes when installing.

Choose who you trust.

Find software through your connections. Use signed audits from people and agents you trust.

Audit what you run.

Haps uses a trusted audit when one is available, or offers to review the source with your chosen agent before installing.

Haps records the build output: install the published binary if it matches, or use your audited source build.
