No package registry account.

People, organizations, and agents publish with a Nostr key. No sign-up or approval required. Package names belong to their publishers.

Catalogs without gatekeepers.

Find signed Nostr package records through relays and Hashtree indexes. Create, mirror and search multiple catalogs. Indexes are identified by their content, so any server or peer can share the same index. Haps checks signatures and content hashes.

Social proof for software.

Find publishers through your connections. See who attested to a release and what they checked. Require attestations from keys you trust.

Signed release attestations.

People and agents can endorse an exact release or warn about a finding. See what was checked and read the signed notes before installing.

Only your key and unmuted keys you directly follow count. Trusted warnings block installation. Set up an agent to scan releases and sign its findings.

How attestations work ↗
SIGN YOUR CLAIM
haps attest alice/hello --version 1.0.0 \
  --note "Built from source; tests pass"
WARN ABOUT A FINDING
haps warn alice/hello --version 1.0.0 \
  --note "Unexpected outbound connection"
REQUIRE TWO TRUSTED ATTESTATIONS
haps install alice/hello \
  --require-attestations 2

Attestations describe what the signer claims to have checked. Haps verifies the signature, not the claimed work.

Binaries or source.

With help from AI, add a missing feature to a desktop tool or improve your Omarchy workflow. Build it for yourself, then share your version.

Use a published binary, or inspect a build recipe from a pinned Git commit. Hashtree Git URLs work through git-remote-htree.

Source build guide ↗
INSPECT FIRST
haps build htree://alice/project \
  --rev FULL_COMMIT_HASH
BUILD WHEN READY
haps build htree://alice/project \
  --rev FULL_COMMIT_HASH \
  --execute --install

Build recipes run with your user permissions. Haps records the source commit; it does not sandbox builds.

Add your package.

Fix an annoyance. Add a feature. Make it work your way. Let others try your improvements and build on them.

Stage your built app and its runtime files, then describe them in haps.toml. Sign your catalog with a Nostr key and share it through Hashtree.

No submission queue. People add your catalog and choose whether to trust your releases.

Publishing guide ↗
CREATE YOUR SIGNING KEY ONCE
haps identity init
PACKAGE AND SHARE YOUR APP
haps init --name my-app \
  --command bin/my-app --payload stage
haps pack haps.toml --payload stage --out catalog
haps publish catalog --name my-packages

The guide covers staging, local testing, installing from your catalog, updates, and setting up htree.
